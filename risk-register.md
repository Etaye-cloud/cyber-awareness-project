Cyber Awareness Risk Register
This risk register is for a fictional warehouse or retail workplace.

Risk	Description	Likelihood	Impact	Recommended Control
Phishing	Employees receive fake emails or text messages designed to steal passwords or sensitive information.	High	High	Security awareness training and phishing reporting procedures
Weak Passwords	Employees use simple or reused passwords that can be guessed or stolen.	Medium	High	Strong password policy and multi-factor authentication (MFA)
Lost or Stolen Devices	Company laptops, tablets, or phones are lost and expose business data.	Medium	High	Device encryption and remote wipe capability
Unauthorized Access	Former employees or unauthorized individuals gain access to systems or facilities.	Medium	High	Access reviews and timely account deactivation
Malware Infection	Employees download malicious software that disrupts operations or steals data.	Medium	High	Endpoint protection software and safe browsing training
